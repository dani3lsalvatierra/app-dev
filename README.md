# Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

## About the Series

**Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba** is a Japanese *dark fantasy action anime* based on the manga by **Koyoharu Gotouge**. The anime is produced by **Ufotable** and first aired in 2019. It follows **Tanjiro Kamado**, a kind-hearted boy who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked and his younger sister is turned into a demon.

- Zenitsu Agatsuma
- Inosuke Hashibira
- Muzan Kibutsuji
  - The main villain

## Why I Like It

1. The animation and fight scenes are stunning.
2. The story is about family, courage and never giving up.
3. The characters are memorable and the music fits every battle.

> A story about a brother who fights through every hardship to protect his sister.
